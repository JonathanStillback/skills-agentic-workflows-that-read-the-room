## Recent items to watch

- **November 18, 2026: [Gemini 4 Neon in GitHub Copilot](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-18-gemini-4-neon-in-github-copilot/).** GitHub's changelog relays Google's claim that Gemini 4 Neon delivers quality close to Gemini 4 Carbon at a lower token cost per task, a useful point of comparison when choosing a model for coding work.
- **October 28, 2026: [GitHub Copilot SDK is becoming Microsoft Copilot SDK](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-github-copilot-sdk-is-becoming-microsoft-copilot-sdk/).** The transition is happening in phases; the announcement says existing users do not need to make immediate changes.
